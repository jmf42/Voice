# Pilot Onboarding Runbook

## Goal
Bring a new business live in under 10 minutes.

## Steps

1. Create tenant and settings (business name, business phone, escalation phone, SLA).
2. Ask business to set forwarding to DispatchOS Twilio number.
3. Keep AI intake enabled in settings.
4. Perform one test call:
   - Confirm job appears in Job Board.
   - Confirm SMS is sent.
   - Confirm urgent phrase triggers escalation.
5. Optional: connect Google Calendar in Settings.
6. Share dispatcher workflow:
   - Open Job Board
   - Confirm time / Call back / Close

## Success checklist

- ON/OFF toggle responds instantly.
- At least one normal call and one urgent call pass acceptance checks.
- Business confirms they received trust-building SMS copy.
